Privacy Policy for Brownpaw

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version

  • Brownpaw is a private logbook. Your runs are visible only to you.
  • We store what you put in the app (your account, your logbook, your starred gauges and alerts) so it syncs between your devices.
  • Your location never leaves your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the Me tab at any time.

1. What we store

Your account

When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your email address, your name and a sign-in identifier. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see Apple's relay address. If you continue without an account, we store only an anonymous identifier. You can add an optional profile photo.

Your logbook

Each run you log: the run, the date, the river flow at the time, and anything you choose to add such as notes, a rating, difficulty and who you paddled with. Logbook entries are private to your account.

Your gauges and alerts

The gauges you star, the flow levels you want to be alerted about, and a push notification token for each device so we can send those alerts.

Runs you add

If you add a run that isn't in the guide, it is stored with your account and is visible only to you.

2. What we don't collect

  • Location. When you allow it, Brownpaw reads your location on your phone, only while the app is open, to suggest which run you're at and to show runs near you. It is not sent to us or stored on our servers.
  • Analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't use analytics SDKs, ad networks or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • Contacts, health data or browsing history.

3. How we use it

Only to run Brownpaw: to show you your logbook on every device you sign in on, to fill in river flows for your runs, and to send the flow alerts you set up. We don't use your data for advertising and don't sell, rent or share it.

4. Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging and Hosting) stores your data and delivers notifications on our behalf.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • OpenStreetMap serves map tiles. Like any website, its servers see your device's IP address when map images load.

River levels come from public data published by the Water Survey of Canada and other gauge operators. No personal data is sent to them.

5. Security and storage

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on Google's servers, and Firestore security rules allow only you to read your logbook. The app also keeps a copy on your device so it works on the river without signal. Our servers may be outside Canada.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete any logbook entry at any time. Me → Delete account permanently deletes your account, logbook, profile photo, starred gauges and alerts. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Brownpaw's access to your Apple ID. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You can see and edit your data in the app. To get a copy of your data, or with any privacy question or request, email us at the address below and we'll reply within 30 days. You can turn off location and notifications in your device settings at any time; the app works without them.

8. Children

Brownpaw is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
