Brownpaw Support

Brownpaw is a logbook and river-level app for whitewater paddlers. Questions, bugs or ideas: email support@brownpaw.app and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Your logbook and starred gauges are saved on your phone. Runs you log on the river are kept on the phone and sync the next time you're online.

Where do the river levels come from?

From public hydrometric data, mostly the Water Survey of Canada. Levels update through the day but can be late, missing or wrong. Always scout and make your own call.

How do I get a flow alert?

Tap the bell on a gauge or run page. Allow notifications when your phone asks. You can turn alerts off with the same bell.

Why does Brownpaw ask for my location?

To suggest which run you're at when you check in, and to show runs near you. It's optional, only used while the app is open, and never leaves your phone.

Who can see my logbook?

Only you. Logbook entries are private to your account.

How do I delete my account?

Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, logbook, profile photo, starred gauges and alerts. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days.

A run or gauge is missing or wrong

Email us with the run name and what's off. You can also add your own run from the Log tab; it stays private to you.
